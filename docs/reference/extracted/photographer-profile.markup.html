Good to know

How far ahead should we book?

Peak season dates go 9 to 12 months out. Write to us as soon as your date is fixed.

When do we receive our photographs?

A preview of 25 frames within a week, the full gallery in 6 weeks, and albums in 12 weeks.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. Travel and stay are quoted separately for events outside Karnataka.

How do payments and cancellations work?

A booking advance holds your date, with the balance due before the wedding. If we cannot cover your date we will say so within a day, and refund any advance in full.

Can we choose the shooting style?

Yes. We can lean documentary, editorial, or film-toned, and we plan it with you on the first call.
